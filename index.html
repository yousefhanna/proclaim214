Changing the clocks hurts people

Not the time we keep. The act of changing it. Every March and every November, the same things happen.

+6%More fatal car crashes in the week after we spring forward
+11%More people diagnosed with depression after we fall back
MondayHeart attacks and workplace injuries both jump on the first one

None of these is huge on its own. But we cause all of them, twice a year, on purpose. In 2020, every MPP in Ontario voted to stop.

Why hasn't it happened?

The law needs one signature from the Attorney General. The government said it would sign when Quebec and New York State stopped changing their clocks too.

New York can't do it. US states need permission from Congress to keep daylight time all year. New York's own law also waits for five other states to go first.

Congress passed a bill in the House in July 2026. It's stuck in the Senate now. The same bill passed the Senate in 2022 and then died.

So Ontario is waiting for New York, which is waiting for five states, which are waiting for Congress. That has been the plan for six years.

Ontario says provinces shouldn't have different rules

In 2025, every province signed a deal: if a product can be sold in one province, it can be sold in all of them. Ontario led the push.

The federal minister explained it with an example. A trucker shouldn't have to move his lights when he crosses a provincial border.

We agreed truckers shouldn't move their headlights. We still make them move their clocks.

In July 2026, Ontario got an A+ from the Canadian Federation of Independent Business for having zero exceptions to free trade with other provinces. Ontario passed its own law to speed this up.

One difference is left that Ontario could fix with a signature. Instead, we're matching New York.

And it's getting worse. From November, Manitoba will be on Ontario's winter time all year. In winter, no time difference. In summer, one hour. Anyone booking trucks or calls across that border now has to check the calendar first. That's a new barrier between two provinces. Ontario made it by doing nothing.

What people say against it

Sleep scientists prefer standard time.

They do. The American Academy of Sleep Medicine and the Canadian Sleep Society both say standard time is better for our bodies. They may be right. But the harm comes from switching. Ontario's law picks daylight time. Quebec's and Manitoba's people picked daylight time. Stop switching first. Argue about which time later.

Winter mornings will be dark.

Yes. In late December, the sun won't rise in Toronto until about 8:50 a.m. That's the real cost. Schools can start later. Six provinces and territories are about to show how it works. MPPs knew this in 2020 and voted yes anyway.

We'll be out of step with the US.

We're already out of step with six Canadian provinces and territories. From November, we'll be out of step with Manitoba half the year. We have to pick who to match. This year, Ontario matched Canada on everything else.
